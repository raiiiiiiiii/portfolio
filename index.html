└ the user gets what was quoted, regardless of liquidity conditions at that moment

The part beginners usually underestimate is what happens before and after the trade

Here is the simple breakdown :

1. Getting money into Binance

If you are in Pakistan P2P is one of the ways to buy crypto

You pay the seller through your chosen payment method while Binance keeps the crypto in escrow until the trade is completed.

Already have crypto else?
You can deposit it directly to your Binance wallet

There is one thing you should never rush:

the network

USDT on TRC20 and USDT on ERC20 are not the same just because both are called USDT

Always check the network shown by Binance before sending anything

2. P2P has one rule

If you are selling crypto:

do not give away crypto because someone sends a screenshot

Check your bank account or payment balance
Screenshots can be edited
Your account balance is what matters.

If you are buying make sure you follow the payment instructions and use traders with a track record.

3. Withdrawing crypto

This is where one small mistake can become expensive

Select your asset

→ enter the wallet address
→ choose the network
→ check the amount
→ confirm

Before confirming check the address and network again

Crypto transactions are generally irreversible once sent

4. Converting crypto back to money

You can also use Binance P2P to sell your crypto and receive payment through the available local payment methods.

Same rule applies:

payment received first crypto released second

The interesting thing is that most beginner mistakes are not really about knowing what button to press

They happen because people move fast

Wrong network.
Wrong address.
Fake payment screenshot.
Rushing through P2P.
Slow down check twice confirm.

In crypto a 10 second check can save you from an expensive mistake
